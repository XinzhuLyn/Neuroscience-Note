## Retina --> Subcortical structure

Glossary for this part

|Eng|Ch||
|-|-|-|
|Binocular|双眼的|Related to or using both eyes|
|Monocular|单眼的|Related to or using one eye|
|Optic chiasm|视交叉|Site where optic nerve partially cross|
|Optic tract|视束|Bundle of nerve fibers after the optic chiasm, projecting to the LGN|
|Temporal hemiretina|颞侧半视网膜|Temporal half of retina, fibers **do not cross** at chiasm|
|Nasal hemiretina|鼻侧半视网膜|Nasal half of retina, fibers **cross** at chiasm|
|Lateral geniculate nucleus, LGN|外侧膝状体|Thalamic visual relay nucleus, receive optic tract, project to V1|
|Striate cortex, V1, Primary visual cortex|纹状皮层，V1，初级视皮层|Primary visual cortex, located in occipital lobe, named for its striated apparance|
|Mesencephalon, Midbrain|菱脑，中脑|Located on the top of the brain stem, includes Tectum|
|Tectum|顶盖|Dorsal midbrain structure, include superior colliculus|
|Superior colliculus|上丘|A part of tectum in midbrain, mediates visual reflexes(Ex.saccades)|
