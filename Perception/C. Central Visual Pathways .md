**There are 6 layers of cells in LGN**

images from wikipedia

```
- M cells projecting to layer 1,2, therefore Layer 1 & 2 are named magnocellular layers(大颗粒细胞层）
- P cells projecting to layer 3,4,5,6, therefore layer 3,4,5,6 are named parvocellular layers(小细胞颗粒层）
- Visual information from ipsilateral hemiretina is project to Layer 2,3,5
- Visual information from contralateral hemiretina is project to Layer 1,4,6
```

**There are two pathways in visual information transduction, where M cells and P cells are still separating**

|Pathways|Initiation point|Function|Orientation|
|--|--|--|--|
|Dorsal pathway|LGN magnocellular layers|Responsible for transmitting depth & motor visual information : Where the object is|To posterior parietal lobe|
|Ventral pathway|LGN parvocellular layers|Responsible for transmitting color & edge(form) information : What the object is|To inferior temporal lobe|

**Summing up the pathway from retina to subcortical area: retina-> optic chiasm-> optic tracts-> lateral geniculate nucleus(LGN)**
