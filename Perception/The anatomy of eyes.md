# The anatomy of eyes

In this note, we will briefly review the anatomic structure of the eyes.

|Structure|Function|
|--|--|
|Cornea角膜|Transparent front surface of the eye; refracts (bends) light entering the eye.|
|Aqueous humor房水|Clear fluid filling the front chamber; nourishes the cornea and lens and maintains intraocular pressure.|
|Iris虹膜|Pigmented muscular ring; controls the size of the pupil to regulate light entry.|
|Pupil瞳孔|Opening in the center of the iris; allows light to pass into the eye.|
|Sclera巩膜|Tough white outer coat; protects the eyeball and maintains its shape|
|Extraocular muscle眼外肌|Muscles attached outside the eyeball; move the eye in different directions.|
|Lens晶状体|Transparent elastic structure; fine-tunes focus by changing shape (accommodation).|
|Ciliary muscle睫状肌| Smooth muscle ring; contracts/relaxes to change lens shape for focusing.|
|Zonule fiber悬韧带| Suspensory ligaments; hold the lens in place and transmit ciliary muscle tension to it.|
|Vitreous humor玻璃体| Clear gel filling the back of the eye; maintains shape and supports the retina.|
|Retina视网膜| Light-sensitive neural layer; converts light into electrical signals.|
|Fovea中央凹|Small central depression in the retina; provides sharpest, most detailed vision.|
|Optic nerve视神经| Bundle of nerve fibers; transmits visual signals from the retina to the brain.|

**Accommodation**:  The eye's ability to adjust its focal power (mainly by changing lens shape) to focus on objects at different distances.

**Visual acuity**: Sharpness or clarity of vision; the ability to resolve fine detail.

**Visual field**: The total area that can be seen at any one time, including central and peripheral vision.
