# Central Visual Pathways

In this Chapter, we will focus on how visual information process leaving the retina.

【Reference: Principles of Neuroscience, 4 ed, Eric Kandel.】

## Overview

**Key Pathway**: Visual field in retina **-->** Subcortical Structure **-->** Striate cortex

|**Table of content**||
|-|-|
|Retina **-->** Subcortical Structure|Optic nerve, Optic chiasm, Optic tract, Tectum, Superior colliculus, LGN|
|Retinotopic organization|Visual field, Nasal/Temporal hemiretina, contralateral representation, magnification factor|
|LGN|6 Layers, M pathway, P pathway|
|LGN **-->** Striate cortex|Optic radiation, Meyer's Loop, Visual field representation|
|Primary Visual cortex(V1)|6 Layers, Layer 4Cα, Layer 4Cβ, M->4Cα, P->4Cβ, orientation columns, ocular dominance columns, blobs, hypercolumns|
|Visual Deficits|Optic nerve lesion, Optic chiasm lesion, Optic tract lesion, Meyer's loop lesion, V1 lesion|
|Key Principles|Retinotopy, Parallel processing, Modular organization, M/P segregation|




## Retina --> Subcortical structure

Glossary for this part

|Eng|Ch||
|-|-|-|
|Binocular|双眼的|Related to or using both eyes|
|Monocular|单眼的|Related to or using one eye|
|Optic chiasm|视交叉|Site where optic nerve partially cross|
|Optic tract|视束|Bundle of nerve fibers after the optic chiasm, projecting to the LGN|
|Temporal hemiretina|颞侧半视网膜|Temporal half of retina, fibers **do not cross** at chiasm|
|Nasal hemiretina|鼻侧半视网膜|Nasal half of retina, fibers **cross** at chiasm|
|Lateral geniculate nucleus, LGN|外侧膝状体|Thalamic visual relay nucleus, receive optic tract, project to V1|
|Striate cortex, V1, Primary visual cortex|纹状皮层，V1，初级视皮层|Primary visual cortex, located in occipital lobe, named for its striated apparance|
|Mesencephalon, Midbrain|菱脑，中脑|Located on the top of the brain stem, includes Tectum|
|Tectum|顶盖|Dorsal midbrain structure, include superior colliculus|
|Superior colliculus|上丘|A part of tectum in midbrain, mediates visual reflexes(Ex.saccades)|
